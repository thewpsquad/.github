## Our products

### 🧩 Squad Modules — the all-in-one Divi plugin

Our flagship: one plugin, a full toolkit of advanced Divi modules and extensions, built for **Divi 4 and Divi 5**. No coding required — just drag, drop, and design.

| | Modules | Extensions | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[Squad Modules Lite](https://wordpress.org/plugins/squad-modules-for-divi/)** — free | 65 | 8 | WordPress.org |
| **[Squad Modules Pro](https://squadmodules.com/pricing/)** — premium | 73 | 11 | squadmodules.com |

The Lite build is free forever on WordPress.org (**1,000+ active installs · 5.0★**) and covers creative, media, dynamic-content, form-styler, and auth modules. Pro adds advanced modules and extensions (Popup Maker, Display Conditions, Scheduled Content, and more), pre-built layouts, and priority support.

**Compatibility:** WordPress 6.0+ · PHP 7.4+ · Divi / Extra / Divi Builder 4.13+ · WPML-ready.

### 🔧 Standalone Divi plugins on WordPress.org

For teams who want just one focused tool instead of the full pack:

| Plugin | What it does |
|---|---|
| **[Squad Form Styler](https://wordpress.org/plugins/form-styler-for-divi/)** | Style Contact Form 7, Gravity Forms, WPForms, and Fluent Forms natively inside Divi's design options. |
| **[Squad Post Grid](https://wordpress.org/plugins/post-grid-module-for-divi/)** | Build flexible, great-looking post grids to showcase your content — Divi 4 and Divi 5. |

## Open source

A few of our repositories are public — mirrors, issue trackers, and reusable tooling:

- **[squad-modules-lite](https://github.com/thewpsquad/squad-modules-lite)** — source mirror and issue tracker for Squad Modules Lite. Bug reports and feature requests welcome here.
- **[freemius-wp-rest](https://github.com/thewpsquad/freemius-wp-rest)** — reusable WordPress REST API proxy routes for the Freemius SDK. Handy for any plugin shipping with Freemius.

Found a bug or have an idea? [Open an issue](https://github.com/thewpsquad/squad-modules-lite/issues) — we read every one.
